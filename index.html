隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日 · 適用版本：1.2.0 以上 · 套件名稱：com.sealstech.parkupin

不需註冊帳號資料只存在你的手機停車資料不上傳含 Google AdMob 廣告可付費去除廣告

「停車小幫手」（以下稱「本 App」）協助你記錄停車位置並導航回到車子旁。我們重視你的隱私，本政策說明本 App 如何處理資料。

1. 我們處理哪些資料

  • 位置資訊：當你按下「記錄停車位置」或開啟地圖頁時，本 App 會取得裝置的 GPS 位置，用於儲存停車地點、顯示你目前的位置、計算距離與步行時間。
  • 停車紀錄：停車座標、時間、所選車款與你輸入的備註（例如樓層、車位），儲存在裝置本機（shared_preferences）。
  • 照片（選用）：若你選擇拍攝停車位置照片，照片只會存放在本 App 於你裝置上的私有資料夾。
  • 車款設定：你選擇或自訂的車款名稱與顏色，同樣只儲存在裝置本機。

2. 停車資料不會離開你的裝置

本 App 沒有帳號系統、沒有自己的伺服器。你的停車位置、紀錄與照片不會上傳給我們，也不會出售、出租或提供給任何第三方（包含廣告商）。本 App 不使用分析／追蹤 SDK；但會顯示 Google AdMob 廣告，詳見下方第 4 節。

3. 第三方地圖服務

  • Android：使用 Google Maps SDK 顯示地圖。Google Maps SDK 載入地圖時，會依 Google 的政策自動收集部分技術資料，例如 IP 位址、裝置與系統版本等請求中繼資料、SDK 當機記錄、Maps SDK 專用的匿名識別碼，以及地圖操作（平移、縮放）事件，用於維運與改善 Google 服務。這些資料由 Google 直接處理，我們無法取得。請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。
  • iOS：使用 Apple 地圖（MapKit）顯示地圖，請參閱 Apple 隱私權政策。
  • 導航：按下「導航」時，本 App 會把停車地點座標交給你裝置上的外部地圖 App（Google 地圖或 Apple 地圖）開啟步行路線；之後的處理適用該 App 的隱私權政策。

4. 廣告（Google AdMob）

本 App 免費提供，並在「首頁」與「紀錄」頁底部顯示橫幅廣告（地圖頁與車款頁不顯示廣告）。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供，載入廣告時 Google Mobile Ads SDK 可能會收集並傳送給 Google 下列資料：

  • 裝置或其他 ID：廣告 ID（Android 廣告 ID／iOS IDFA，iOS 須經你在追蹤授權視窗同意）、App 組 ID。
  • 大概位置：由 IP 位址推估的大略位置。本 App 不會把 GPS 精確位置或停車位置提供給廣告 SDK。
  • App 互動：廣告的曝光與點擊、App 啟動等。
  • 診斷資料：當機記錄、效能資料。

這些資料由 Google 依其政策用於放送、衡量與改善廣告（可能包含個人化廣告），以及防範詐欺與濫用；我們不會取得可識別你個人身分的資料。詳見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式中的資訊。

同意與選擇：在法規要求的地區（例如歐洲經濟區、英國、瑞士），本 App 會在載入廣告前透過 Google 使用者訊息平台（UMP）徵求你的同意；你可隨時在 App「設定與關於 → 廣告隱私設定」中變更選擇。你也可以在 Android「設定 → Google → 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID，或在 iOS「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」關閉追蹤。

5. 應用程式內購買（去除廣告）

你可以在「設定與關於」頁一次性購買「去除廣告」，購買後即不再載入廣告。付款由 Google Play 或 Apple App Store 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資訊（例如信用卡號）。本 App 只會在裝置本機記錄「已去除廣告」狀態，並向商店查詢購買紀錄以驗證／還原購買；商店依其政策保存你的購買紀錄（Google Payments 隱私權聲明、Apple 隱私權政策）。

6. 權限說明

  • 位置（使用 App 期間）：記錄停車位置與顯示目前位置。本 App 不會在背景追蹤你的位置。
  • 相機（選用）：只在你主動拍攝停車照片時使用。
  • 廣告 ID（Android：com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID）：供 Google AdMob 放送與衡量廣告。
  • App 追蹤透明度（iOS）：詢問是否允許使用 IDFA 提供個人化廣告；拒絕不影響 App 功能。
  • 網路：載入地圖、廣告與處理購買。

你可以隨時在系統設定中關閉上述權限。

7. 資料保存與刪除

你可以在 App 內「紀錄」頁刪除任何停車紀錄或清除全部紀錄；移除本 App 時，所有本機資料（包含照片）會一併刪除。購買紀錄由 Google Play／App Store 保存，重新安裝後可用「還原購買」恢復。Google 收集的廣告相關資料依 Google 的保存政策處理。

8. 兒童隱私

本 App 不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，我們也不會蒐集任何可識別個人身分的資料。

9. 政策變更

若本政策有所更新，將於此頁面公告並更新生效日期。若有重大變更，我們會在 App 中告知。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Applies to version 1.2.0 and later · Package: com.sealstech.parkupin

No accountData stays on your deviceParking data never uploadedAds by Google AdMobOptional purchase to remove ads

ParkuPin (停車小幫手, "the App") helps you remember where you parked and navigate back to your vehicle. This policy explains how the App handles data.

1. Data the App processes

  • Location: when you tap "Save parking location" or open the map, the App reads your device's GPS location to save the parking spot, show your current position, and estimate distance and walking time.
  • Parking records: coordinates, time, selected vehicle and any note you type (e.g. floor or bay number) are stored locally on your device (shared_preferences).
  • Photos (optional): if you take a photo of your parking spot, it is stored only in the App's private folder on your device.
  • Vehicle settings: the vehicles you select or create (name and color) are stored locally.

2. Your parking data never leaves your device

The App has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your parking locations, history and photos are not sent to us and are not sold, rented or shared with any third party (including advertisers). The App uses no analytics/tracking SDK, but it does show Google AdMob ads — see section 4.

3. Third-party map services

  • Android: maps are displayed with the Google Maps SDK. When maps load, the Google Maps SDK automatically collects certain technical data under Google's policies — such as IP address, request metadata (device model, OS and SDK version), SDK crash logs, a pseudonymous Maps SDK identifier, and map interaction events (panning, zooming) — to operate and improve Google services. This data goes directly to Google; we do not receive it. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • iOS: maps are displayed with Apple Maps (MapKit) — see the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Navigation: tapping "Navigate" passes the parking coordinates to an external map app on your device (Google Maps or Apple Maps) to show walking directions; that app's privacy policy then applies.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows a banner ad at the bottom of the Home and History screens (never on the Map or Vehicles screens). Ads are served by Google AdMob. When ads load, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and send the following to Google:

  • Device or other IDs: advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA — on iOS only if you allow tracking) and app set ID.
  • Approximate location: coarse location derived from your IP address. The App never passes your precise GPS or parking location to the ads SDK.
  • App interactions: ad impressions and clicks, app launches.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data.

Google uses this data under its policies to serve, measure and improve ads (which may be personalized) and to prevent fraud and abuse; we do not receive personally identifiable information. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and choices: where required by law (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland) the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before loading ads, and you can change your choice any time under Settings & About → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. In-app purchase (Remove ads)

You can make a one-time "Remove ads" purchase on the Settings & About screen; after purchase no ads are loaded. Payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store — we never receive your payment details (e.g. card number). The App only stores a local "ads removed" flag and queries the store for your purchase to verify/restore it; the store keeps your purchase history under its own policy (Google Payments Privacy Notice, Apple Privacy Policy).

6. Permissions

  • Location (while using the App): to save your parking spot and show your position. The App does not track your location in the background.
  • Camera (optional): only when you choose to take a parking photo.
  • Advertising ID (Android: com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): asks whether the IDFA may be used for personalized ads; declining does not affect any feature.
  • Internet: to load maps and ads and to process purchases.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in system settings.

7. Retention and deletion

You can delete individual parking records or clear all history in the App's History tab. Uninstalling the App deletes all locally stored data, including photos. Purchase records are kept by Google Play / the App Store and can be restored with "Restore purchase" after reinstalling. Ad-related data collected by Google is handled under Google's retention policies.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not collect personally identifiable information.

9. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We will notify you in the App of any material change.
